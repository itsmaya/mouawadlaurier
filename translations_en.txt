── PAGE RISING ──
r-intro = "An outdoor sound and light installation speaking of our vulnerability through a poetic experience. Pushing fear aside, using beauty to become more resilient."
r-work-title = Visualising rising sea levels
r-work-1 = Rising is an immersive outdoor installation translating scientific data on rising sea levels into a sensory experience. Light waves submerge the space in real time.
r-work-2 = The installation connects in real time to oceanographic data from the University of La Rochelle. Greater climate anomalies make the experience more intense.
r-work-q = "When art meets science. Creating new narratives about the ocean, in service of the living."
r-int-title = Making the invisible visible
r-int-1 = Rising speaks of our vulnerability through a poetic experience. The goal: break the taboo of the future ahead, pushing fear aside and using beauty to become more resilient.
r-int-2 = Climate data is not abstract · it is lived, felt in the body. Rising transforms knowledge into consciousness.
r-pq = "Making the invisible visible: translating climate-driven transformations into experiences that mobilise."

── PAGE PEUPLER ──
p-intro = "Every living entity is part of a people and populates the world in its own way."
p-work-title = A living room, in real time
p-work-1 = Peupler is housed in a new kind of immersive room made up entirely of real-time generative content. Its content adapts to the data it receives and to the presence of its spectators.
p-work-2 = Spatialised music and olfactory experiences reinforce the bond between nature and humans.
p-work-q = "Peupler poetically explores the human-nature dialectic." · Kent Bye, Voices of VR
p-int-title = Giving the tree a voice
p-int-1 = This installation seeks to see and feel the perspective of a tree observing our cities.
p-int-2 = The cities of tomorrow must consider cohabitation with nature as an absolute necessity.
p-pq = "How can all living beings · humans, non-humans · cohabit in shared territories?"

── PAGE RETOUR AUX SOURCES ──
ret-intro = "A root planted in a chalk cave thirty metres underground. In this mineral darkness, it listens to the living world breathing at the surface."
ret-work-title = A root that listens to the climate
ret-work-1 = The root listens to the natural elements involved in champagne production. In real time, it captures climate data and translates these variations into generative music and light.
ret-work-2 = A permanent installation visible during visits to the Ruinart cellars in Reims since 2019.
ret-work-q = "The ocean projected on the walls is a sublime reminder of the oceanic origin of chalk." · Numéro Art
ret-int-title = Humility before deep time
ret-int-1 = These caves invite us to put our human ego in its place, to realise that we are nothing against the deep time of the Earth.
ret-int-2 = Inspired by Stefano Mancuso on plant intelligence, the installation reminds us that 90% of Earth's biomass consists of plants.
ret-pq = "Technology in service of contemplation, not domination."

── PAGE ÉPHÉMÈRE ──
e-intro = "The present moment as a living work. Éphémère speaks of the time that unites us and separates us."
e-work-title = A painting generated by presence
e-work-1 = An interactive device that, through the mere presence of visitors, generates a living painting and evolving music. The screen responds to all types of movement via a depth sensor.
e-work-2 = The visuals change continuously. The music creates a meditative atmosphere. Accessible to all through simple gesture.
e-work-q = "Allowing a person, simply through their presence, to create a painting in motion."
e-int-title = Art as care
e-int-1 = Éphémère transforms care spaces into places of contemplation. A timeless interlude in hospital waiting rooms.
e-int-2 = Children can engage regardless of their motor or communication abilities. Digital art in service of social cohesion.
e-pq = "These installations bring moments of play, imagination and emotional relief to environments that can otherwise feel stressful."

── PAGE PAYSAGE SONORE ──
ps-intro = "A hypnotic musical harmony links the real and the virtual · particles of light react to a composition generated in real time by the sounds of all visitors."
ps-work-title = Visitors' sounds as a score
ps-work-1 = Paysage Sonore is a musical performance in augmented reality, generated in real time by the sounds of all visitors.
ps-work-2 = Particles of light react to this composition. Each visitor becomes, unknowingly, a composer of the collective experience.
ps-int-title = The collective as instrument
ps-int-1 = Paysage Sonore transforms collective presence into music, revealing that each gesture contributes to a greater whole.
ps-int-2 = It is an experience of collective consciousness · nature as a score, humans as unwitting musicians.
ps-pq = "Connecting the real and the virtual through sound · each visitor becomes a composer without knowing it."

── PAGE ÊTRE ABSORBÉ ──
ab-intro = "Nature is not foreign to us. It perceives us, responds to us and integrates us."
ab-work-title = The visitor becomes an actor
ab-work-1 = Être Absorbé is the continuation of Retour aux Sources. The visitor does not merely contemplate, but becomes an actor.
ab-work-2 = The person becomes one with the root through their movements. It is about communicating with it, feeling its strength.
ab-work-q = "Like a drop absorbed by the immensity of the sea, the visitor discovers both vulnerability and immense strength."
ab-int-title = Union with living matter
ab-int-1 = Être Absorbé invites movement and interaction. Reconnecting with our roots, no longer observing the exterior as strange.
ab-int-2 = There are no Humans opposed to Nature · but a whole, and only a whole.
ab-pq = "Being absorbed by the Source, like a drop inevitably swallowed by the rest of the water."

── PAGE UPPERGROUND ──
ug-intro = "Recalling nature in the place where the user is most removed from it. The lights react to wind conditions in real time."
ug-work-title = Wind as raw material
ug-work-1 = Upperground recalls nature in the place where the user is most removed from it. St Pancras is one of the most urban environments.
ug-work-2 = Upperground uses the wind · a living thing · to create a slow contemplative light animation, contrasting with urban life.
ug-work-q = "Robotisation of humanity is a way to forget we are part of nature, and to forget our responsibility to protect it."
ug-pq = "Recalling nature where humans are most removed from it · using wind as the sole raw material."

── PAGE ALBATROS ──
al-intro = "5 years without touching land. 150,000 km flown per year. 75 years of life. Showing this animal life-size, in paper."
al-work-title = A life-size bird, in paper
al-work-1 = The albatross spends 80% of its time between water and air. It only sets foot on land to reproduce on its island of birth.
al-work-2 = A life-size paper albatross, made using a technique combining origami and 3D modelling · open source, light, recyclable.
al-work-q = "Technology to attract the gaze and redirect it towards Nature." · Mouawad + Laurier
al-int-title = Democratising science through art
al-int-1 = In collaboration with CNRS scientists, their work on albatrosses becomes an accessible artistic experience.
al-int-2 = The travelling NanoMuseum brings science to remote places, schools, public spaces.
al-pq = "Art must not remain locked away. The NanoMuseum is a beautiful way to take it out."
