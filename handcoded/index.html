Expertise

  • Imagine · Concepts for brands and venues
    For a brand, a launch or a venue, we invent the experience: what the audience will see, hear and set off. We write the concept, the device and the way it responds, then we build it all the way to opening day.
  • Explore · Consulting and workshops
    You are looking for what is possible before knowing what you want. We bring your teams together, put the constraints on the table, test leads and help you pick the right one, even if it means steering the project…
  • Build · Development and production
    You come with a brief or a concept already written. Real-time software, sensors, light, sound, electronics: we build the device from end to end, and run it on site or on tour.
  • Support · Technical support for agencies and artists
    An agency or an artist has the project in mind. We give them the tools to make it exist, from prototype to installation, without taking their place: your idea, your signature, our workshop.
  • Concept design
    We join the brainstorm early, suggest the technologies that serve the idea, and carry it from concept to production.
  • Project management
    One team from the first sketch to the last show, with artists, agencies and brands. Around Maya and Cyril, we bring together for each project the collaborators, specialists and craftspeople it needs.
  • Sustainable thinking
    Sustainable design approaches in the choice of materials, energy and durability of every installation.
  • Machine learning & our own AI
    Systems that listen, watch and decide: emotion detection in music, real-time choices on stage, recommendation engines.
  • Real-time development
    Software that reacts within the frame: custom engines in C++, openFrameworks, OpenGL and shaders, built to run night after night.
  • Audio processing
    Sound analysis and generation that drive light, image and motion: beat detection, spatialisation, interactive music.
  • Generative animation
    Algorithms that generate designs and animations, for LEDs, screens and projection.
  • Projection mapping
    Projection on architecture, objects and bodies, synchronised with sound and sensors.
  • Light and LED control
    From a few strips to thousands of LEDs and motors, designed and driven with Ledotron, over DMX and Art-Net.
  • Tracking and sensors
    Kinect, Leap Motion, cameras, body and brain sensors: we turn people's presence and movement into data.
  • Our tools
    Autodesk Maya, After Effects, TouchDesigner, Photoshop, Xcode, MadMapper, Millumin, Ableton Live and our own Ledotron. C++, OpenGL, openFrameworks, Objective-C, SceneKit, Python, Max/MSP, GLSL, VST and WebGL. Ledotron,…
  • Hardware and protocols
    DMX, Art-Net, OSC, Kinect 1 and 2, Leap Motion, augmented reality, iOS, Android and Arduino.

    We design and build immersive spaces where image, light and sound are generated in real time, so the work is never the same twice. Cameras and sensors follow the visitors, live data from the climate, the music or the…
