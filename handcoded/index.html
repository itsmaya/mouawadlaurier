Hand Coded

Hand Coded is a Barcelona studio that designs and builds interactive art installations, immersive spaces and real-time software: light, sound, AI and our own tools.

Retour aux Sources

Mouawad Laurier · 2019 · Maison Ruinart, Reims, UNESCO World Heritage

A root planted in a chalk cellar dug more than two thousand years ago, thirty metres underground. In this mineral darkness, it listens to the living world breathing on the surface. The root listens to the natural elements involved in making champagne. In real time it captures climate data, temperature, humidity, rainfall and wind, and turns these variations into generative music and light. Inspired by Stefano…

Éphémère

Maya Mouawad, with Art Explora · 2023 · Fondation OVE Paris, Sant Joan de Déu Barcelona, CHU Lille

The present moment as a living artwork. Through their simple presence, visitors create a moving painting and an evolving music. A depth sensor follows every kind of movement, with no instructions and no prerequisites: anyone can play, whatever their motor or communication abilities. Éphémère turns hospital waiting rooms and corridors into places of contemplation and play. Permanent at the Fondation OVE in Paris…

Tientos al tiempo

Pablo Valbuena & Patricia Guerrero · 2020 · CENTQUATRE, Paris

A flamenco performance where light becomes a stage object. We built the real-time sound analysis and the performance setup that let the light answer the dancer and the voice. “I want to use the light as an object, not as a way to display the performer.” Pablo Valbuena Produced by the CENTQUATRE in Paris. Several dates were cancelled because of the Covid restrictions.

Maestro

Mouawad Laurier & Tigre Lab · 2017 · Sharjah Light Festival, UAE

Three interactive experiences projected on a 500-metre-wide monument. Visitors touch a physical model of the building, and their gestures play sound and image on the façade. SAND: move sand particles and compose a desert soundscape. ORGAN: become the DJ of an interactive looper that triggers video and audio loops. MOSAIC: reshape the forms and colours of Islamic mosaics. Sharjah Light Festival, 2 to 11 February 2017.

MGNTRN

Romain Tardy · 2014 · PROYECTA, Puebla, Mexico

An immersive show built around a Ferris wheel, projection screens and 10-metre LED strips, all synchronised with the music. We wrote the software to previsualise, animate and control every light from a single timeline, and built a robot that turns the Ferris wheel's control knobs in response to musical events, driven from an iPad or from the Ledotron timeline. PROYECTA festival, Puebla, Mexico, 9 to 11 May 2014.

Viktoria Modesta

Music Tech Fest performance · 2016 · Music Tech Fest, Berlin

Viktoria Modesta is a singer whose bionic leg is at the heart of her shows. For Music Tech Fest 2016 she challenged a team of hackers: let her control music and lights with her leg and arm movements, her voice and her brain. The show was built in under a week: leg and body sensors, interactive music, interactive projection mapping, costume lighting, LED skin and brain sensors. Presented in Berlin on 28 May 2016.

Audi Mind Race

Or how to get focused · 2013 · Camp Nou, Barcelona

Barça players race Scalextric cars with their minds. A MindWave headset reads each player's brain activity and drives the car. We connected the brain data to the cars, and designed a system that maps dynamic visuals onto the track, following the cars with a custom tracking system. Camp Nou, December 2013.

Sonaris

AI music search engine

A music recommendation engine. Sonaris analyses an audio stream in real time and finds the tracks that sound like it, whatever their name or origin. Discover new artists, build well-mixed playlists, license music for film and advertising in minutes. “I like it: is there anything else like it?” Michela Magas. The technology is protected by patent WO2014096832A1.

Upperground

Maya Mouawad, music by Cyril Laurier · 2018 · Lumiere London, St Pancras

Bringing nature back where people are furthest from it. St Pancras may be one of the most urban places there is, where control and speed are everything. In the tunnel, the lights are connected to the wind and react to it in real time. The wind, a living thing acting in the space, is the only raw material: it drives a slow, contemplative light animation, in contrast with city life. Lumiere London festival, 2018.

Expertise

Imagine

Artistic and advertising concepts

We start from an intuition, a brand or a place, and invent the experience: what the audience will see, hear and set off. We write the concept, the device and the way it responds.

Explore

Consulting and workshops

You are looking for what is possible before knowing what you want. We bring your teams together, put the constraints on the table, test leads and help you pick the right one, even if it means steering the project elsewhere.

Build

Development and production

Real-time software, sensors, light, sound, electronics: we build the device from end to end, and run it on site or on tour.

Support

Technical support for artists

An artist has the work in mind. We give them the tools to make it exist, from prototype to installation, without taking their place.

Machine learning & our own AI

Systems that listen, watch and decide: emotion detection in music, real-time choices on stage, recommendation engines.

Real-time development

Software that reacts within the frame: custom engines in C++, openFrameworks, OpenGL and shaders, built to run night after night.

Audio processing

Sound analysis and generation that drive light, image and motion: beat detection, spatialisation, interactive music.

Generative animation

Algorithms that generate designs and animations, for LEDs, screens and projection.

Projection mapping

Projection on architecture, objects and bodies, synchronised with sound and sensors.

Light and LED control

From a few strips to thousands of LEDs and motors, designed and driven with Ledotron, over DMX and Art-Net.

Tracking and sensors

Kinect, Leap Motion, cameras, body and brain sensors: we turn people's presence and movement into data.

Concept design

We join the brainstorm early, suggest the technologies that serve the idea, and carry it from concept to production.

Sustainable thinking

Sustainable design approaches in the choice of materials, energy and durability of every installation.

Project management

One team from the first sketch to the last show, with artists, agencies and brands.

Our tools

Autodesk Maya, After Effects, TouchDesigner, Photoshop, Xcode, MadMapper, Millumin, Ableton Live and our own Ledotron. C++, OpenGL, openFrameworks, Objective-C, SceneKit, Python, Max/MSP, GLSL, VST and WebGL.

Hardware and protocols

DMX, Art-Net, OSC, Kinect 1 and 2, Leap Motion, augmented reality, iOS, Android and Arduino.
