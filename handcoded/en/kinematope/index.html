Kinematope

Pablo Valbuena, Paisaje Técnico · a tool to drive 5,000 LEDs · 2016 · Espacio Fundación Telefónica, Madrid

Our role Creation and control tools, light and LEDs, engineering.

A circuit of light and sound runs along the gallery’s false ceiling. Animated light and shadow reveal the architecture and offer a new way of experiencing space: “I don’t work in physical space, but in the mental space of the viewer.”

We designed the tool that lets the artist drive 5,000 LEDs with freedom and precision, so the shadows fill the space exactly as he imagined, and so he can adjust every sequence on site, during installation.

“Can we make it faster and slower at the same time?” Pablo Valbuena

Paisaje Técnico exhibition, Espacio Fundación Telefónica, Madrid, 18 March to 15 May 2016.
