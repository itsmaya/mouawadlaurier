Complete Revolution

Residency with Raquel Gualtero · 2025 · Barcelona

Our role Research residency, interactive projections, rotation.

Complete Revolution is a dance piece by Raquel Gualtero, a dance of continuous rotation in which the dancer spins on herself. Rotation, a simple and universal movement, becomes a gesture of resistance and inner transformation: a revolution that begins and ends at the same point.

The piece unfolds in three parts, carried by a single body: first close to the ground, then the heart of the work, centred on rotation, and finally the body dissolving into a cosmic image. It grows out of 360°, a short piece for public space.

During a residency with Raquel, we worked on visuals and interactions around rotation: projections that respond to the dancer’s movement as she spins. This research was never shown as such. It gave rise to Aurora, a new Mouawad + Laurier work, currently in production.

The piece premiered at the 2025 Grec Festival de Barcelona, at La Caldera, and was then presented at Roca Umbert, in Granollers, as part of the Panoràmic festival.
