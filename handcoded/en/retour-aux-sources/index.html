Retour aux Sources

Maison Ruinart · an artwork by Mouawad + Laurier · 2019 · Maison Ruinart, Reims, UNESCO World Heritage

Our role Concept, sculpture, kinetics, generative music, light and LEDs, real-time software, installation, maintenance.

A root planted in a chalk cellar dug more than two thousand years ago, thirty metres underground. In that mineral darkness, it listens to the living world breathing at the surface.

In real time, the root captures the climate data that makes champagne, temperature, humidity, rainfall and wind, and translates these variations into generative music and light. It moves with the seasons and the cycle of the vine. Inspired by Stefano Mancuso’s work on plant intelligence, it reminds us that 90% of the Earth’s biomass is made of plants.

Two years of work, from concept to finished piece: the dramaturgy, the steel sculpture and its blown-glass lamps, the kinetics, the generative music, the light and the brain that ties it all to weather data. An installation built to run on its own, every day, for ten years: it starts, monitors itself and restarts without anyone on site.

First “carte blanche” commission of the countdown to Maison Ruinart’s tricentenary in 2029. Permanent installation in the chalk cellars since September 2019, seen by around 26,000 visitors a year. Every piece of glass was hand-blown by master glassmaker Marco Nason during a residency at the NasonMoretti glassworks in Murano. The whole electronic system of the lamps was invented and designed by us.
