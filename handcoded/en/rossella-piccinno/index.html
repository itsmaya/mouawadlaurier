Rossella Piccinno

Artist and filmmaker

Italian artist born in 1978 in Salento, based in the Lille area. Trained in documentary and experimental cinema at the University of Bologna, then at Le Fresnoy, she works with film and installation, shown in Europe, the United States and the Middle East. She also teaches photography and image-making.

With us: Eruption of the end, Friends of Le Fresnoy award for best installation in 2011.
