Gyrotope

Pablo Valbuena · light and sound spatialisation · 2016 · KM1 Grand Paris Express, Paris · Nuit Blanche, Brussels

Our role Creation and control tools, light and LEDs, sound.

A large circle of columns forms a sculptural volume that unfolds in time. Light and sound are its material, and it applies the sequencing of cinema to space. Walk around it: it is an object; step inside: it becomes an environment.

We designed the light and sound spatialisation system: each column is both a light source and a loudspeaker, and everything is animated and triggered from a single timeline, so the work plays identically every night, outdoors.

Commissioned by Grand Paris Express for KM1, the launch celebration of the construction works, Paris, 5 June 2016, then Nuit Blanche Brussels, 1 October 2016.
