Hors du temps

Mouawad + Laurier, with Art Explora · 2023 · Centre Robert Doisneau Paris, Sant Joan de Déu Barcelona, Lille University Hospital

Our role Concept, tracking, generative visuals, sound, installation.

The present moment as a living artwork. Simply by being there, visitors create a moving picture and an evolving music. No instructions, no prerequisites: anyone can draw and compose the music, whatever their motor or communication abilities.

A depth sensor follows every movement in the room. We designed the generative image, the interactive music and the integration into places of care: a screen, a sensor, a small computer, and the installation runs on its own, every day, with no dedicated staff. Made for children, families and carers, it works just as well in a waiting room as in a corridor.

Hors du temps turns hospital waiting rooms and corridors into places of contemplation and play. Permanent at the Centre Robert Doisneau of the OVE Foundation in Paris since June 2023 and at the Sant Joan de Déu children’s hospital in Barcelona since December 2023, where it welcomes children in outpatient palliative care. Lille University Hospital in 2026, then Bordeaux University Hospital. Presented at Museum Connections 2026.
