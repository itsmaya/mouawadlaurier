Cupra, giant LED screen

Cupra · real-time tracking for a giant LED screen · Berlin

Our role Tracking, real-time software, engineering.

A giant LED screen that watches passers-by. Their shadows are embedded into the image in real time and blend with the brand film: the wall comes alive, with no instructions needed.

For Cupra in Berlin, we developed the tracking system and its link to the content on screen: a camera, live analysis of the silhouettes and their integration into the video wall.
