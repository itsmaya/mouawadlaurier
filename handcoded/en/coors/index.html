Coors

Choose your beer · gesture interface · 2015 · New York

Our role Concept, tracking, real-time software.

A Kinect interface to pick your beer by hand: stand in front of the screen, point, and the menu answers the gesture.

We developed the whole interaction system, the interface and the animations, with one simple requirement: it has to work every time, for anyone, smoothly, in a public place where nobody reads instructions.
