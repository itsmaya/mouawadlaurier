Nokia Navigator

Fat-Man Collective with Work Club · 2008 · London

Our role Interactive website, design.

An interactive video player for Nokia Maps, with the agency Work Club: the visitor guides a lost character across Canvey Island, in Essex, while the Nokia Personal Navigators show how to find your way with the phone.
