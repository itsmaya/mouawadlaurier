Wizz

Voting by dancing with your smartphone in the club · 2013 · Paris, France

Our role Animation of all the content, in the app and on the screens projected in the club. Technical direction: collecting data from the smartphones, keeping the phones connected, and gesture voting using their accelerometers.

WIZZWORLD turns a club night into a techno-social game: the crowd becomes interactive. With a smartphone app, everyone dances to the beat following a choreographic language, and directly shapes the atmosphere of the night through different game modes. Players influence the DJ’s music, the VJ’s visuals and the lights, and take part in real-time social interactions.

The first version proved itself at La Machine du Moulin Rouge and at Le 104 in Paris, as part of the Futur en Seine 2012 festival, with the support of the Île-de-France region.
