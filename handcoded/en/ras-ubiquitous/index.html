RAS Ubiquitous

Mouawad + Laurier · Ars in Campania exhibition, Maison Ruinart collection · 2026 – 2027 · Le Cellier, Reims

Our role Generative artwork, sound and light.

A generative artwork by Mouawad + Laurier, from the Maison Ruinart collection, shown in the exhibition Ars in Campania, contemporary artists’ views on nature in Champagne. It turns data into sound and light, and gives a sensory form to the changes in the climate, in the “Climate futures” chapter.

An exhibition by the City of Reims at Le Cellier, a former Maison Mumm shipping cellar built in 1898, from 24 October 2026 to 28 February 2027, curated by Alice Audouin, founder of Art of Change 21, and Oscar Bardet. The exhibition walks through Champagne as a living body, from the chalk subsoil to the cultivated landscapes and on to the futures already surfacing, in three chapters: “Geological times”, “Plant variations” and “Climate futures”. Twenty-two artists, including Julian Charrière, Eva Jospin, Tadashi Kawamata, Vik Muniz, Tomás Saraceno, David Shrigley.

On view until 28 February 2027 at Le Cellier, 4 bis rue de Mars, Reims. Free entry, Tuesday to Sunday from 2 pm to 6 pm, and also Saturday from 10 am to 12 pm. Closed on public holidays and from 21 December to 4 January.
