Maya Benaïnous

Co-founder. Artist and engineer, creator of Ledotron. · Barcelona

Artist and engineer, she creates immersive installations at the crossroads of ecology, technology and the senses, and has worked with real-time data since 2008. She invented a new way to work with LEDs: Ledotron. With Cyril, she signs her artworks as Mouawad + Laurier.

Trained in mathematics, biology and art and technology (IMAC engineering school), she also studied acting in Paris. She began in research and development for cinema special effects at Duboi Duran, made two animated short films for the RAI and interactive web works with the Fat-Man Collective, then set up and art-directed a 3D animation studio for BRB International in Barcelona (104 episodes).

Laureate of the Villa Al Qamar residency of the Institut Français du Liban in 2024, in residency at ElClimamola, Barcelona, in 2025.

“Technology is a tool, nature is the path.”
