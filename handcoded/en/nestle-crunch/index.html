Nestlé Crunch, Human Beats

Nestlé Crunch, Human Beats · interactive installation · 2015 · Palacio de los Deportes, Mexico City

Our role Tracking, sound, generative visuals, real-time software.

Si suena es Crunch. An interactive installation where visitors’ movements generate beats and particles on a giant screen: the body becomes an instrument, with sounds by Camilo Lara (Instituto Mexicano del Sonido).

We developed the interactive core of the piece: body tracking with a Kinect, the interaction of the particles with movement and their animation, for a system that cycles participants through by the minute, in the middle of an event.

Launched for Nestlé México at the Palacio de los Deportes in Mexico City, in October 2015, with an app and a website.
