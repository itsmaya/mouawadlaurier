Ledotron

Our tool for creating and driving light · 2015 – 2026 · From Mexico to China

Ledotron is the tool we wrote for our own installations. It has run more than forty of them since 2015, from OX to Retour aux Sources. LED strips, moving lights, video walls and the venue itself are drawn in 3D; the show is composed on a timeline, and you see the result before a single cable is pulled.

Three ways to play. On loop, a written timeline runs during opening hours, locked, and restarts by itself if anything goes wrong. By draw, the Brain composes a new show at every press, every hour or every message, from the weather, the season and the date: every visit is unique. Live, the Brain listens to the music and makes the image evolve as much as the music does.

The AI that listens to music comes from Cyril Laurier’s PhD thesis on automatic music mood classification at the Music Technology Group of Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcelona (2011). Beats and onsets are detected with aubio, the open-source library born from Paul Brossier’s thesis at Queen Mary University of London.

Inputs: live audio, local weather and climate, clock and calendar, OSC, buttons and keys, Chataigne. Outputs: Art-Net and DMX, video walls, motors, OSC to Ableton Live. Twenty-three generative effects, all able to react to sound or weather. On site: auto-start, locked screen, logging, automatic reconnection and restart. That is what lets us promise a client that an installation will hold for ten years without a technician on site.

Ledotron can be rented or bought, for an installation, a tour or a permanent venue: write to us for a demo or a quote.
