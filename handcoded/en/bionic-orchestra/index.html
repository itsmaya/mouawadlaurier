Bionic Orchestra

Ezra, Organic Orchestra · technologies for live beatbox · 2008 – 2015 · Fontevraud Abbey, Vivoin Abbey, tour

Our role Design, electronics, sound, real-time software.

Technologies for live beatbox, developed with Ezra and the Organic Orchestra company over two residencies a year from 2008 to 2012, with engineers, visual and music creators and researchers from the Music Technology Group of Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcelona. Part of the residencies took place in the CEA’s Art & Science programme in Grenoble.

An electronic glove that drives the whole performance, a looper for a human orchestra, automatic 7.1 sound spatialisation, real-time projections triggered by the music and motion detection. We developed the software chain linking the glove, the voice, the sound and the image, with Ableton Live, Max/MSP, openFrameworks, Kinect, iPhone and iPad, Wii and Arduino.

A week-long residency at Fontevraud Abbey was devoted to sound-driven interactive mapping and to the looper, Maya Benaïnous on projection, Cyril Laurier on sound. Presented at Music Tech Fest London in 2013, and played in concerts and workshops around the world.

In 2015, a long residency at Vivoin Abbey brought together thirty artists living on site in caravans around augmented paper: exploring the boundaries between physical matter, print and digital interaction, with school visits, workshops and performances for the local public. Maya Benaïnous built a motorised kinetic sculpture there.

“I want the audience to get lost between what I do thanks to the machine and what I can really do organically.” Ezra
